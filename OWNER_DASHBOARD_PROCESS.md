# Combined owner dashboard — 20 October 2026

Sign in as the owner, then choose Both stations · Owner dashboard from the sidebar or organisation chooser. Routes are /owner/ and /station2/owner/. Choose a business date and Refresh. Open either station using its button to continue normal entries in that organisation.

Both independent services compute their own summary. Only an active superuser may view the combined dashboard; a matching active superuser must exist in the other station. Short-lived signed requests include target station, originating station, owner username and selected date. The internal endpoint rejects missing, invalid, wrong-target, expired and non-owner requests. Transport uses fixed Docker-service addresses, a three-second timeout, bounded response size and no redirects. Responses are private/no-store. No direct access to the other station database is granted.

Selected-day figures: net fuel/lube sales plus other income after discounts, petrol/diesel litres, lube sales and discounts. Through-date figures: recorded cash/bank, customer balance and estimated debt older than 30 days, supplier payable, unmatched digital gross collections, unallocated bank sources, shortage after waivers/returns and book tank balances. Settlement date cutoff avoids treating future settlements as received. Unallocated settlements stay out of the bank account balance. Cash and bank are recorded subledgers, not externally verified balances. Debt ageing is oldest-first; payment terms are not configured, so this is not a contractual-overdue figure. Shortages may already be held in salary and are not a second demand for money.

Each service reads in a local transaction. The two snapshots have separate visible timestamps and are not globally simultaneous. If a service fails or denies owner access, show its error and the available station data; combined totals are withheld rather than filling missing data with zero. Missing opening accounts are warned as provisional. Historical views use currently active records through the date and can reflect later reversals; approved daily-close records preserve original review history.
